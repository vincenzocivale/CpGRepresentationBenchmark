## What this does and does not support

- The TCGA ordering `regulatory_histone_dnase_v1 > CpGPT-large > DeepCpG-DNA` is reproduced in direction and with paired CIs excluding 0 on this
  independent cohort (validation split).
- The near-parity with `functional_annotations_pca` is also observed: the paired difference is small and its CI includes 0 (it does not show a
  difference; it does not establish equivalence).

## Caveats (carry over from the protocol, plus run-level observations)

- **Validation only, one split, 66 patients.** The external test split is still blocked; this is not the confirmation. Validation also selects the
  checkpoint (small optimism, equal across arms).
- **Budget-limited training.** The best epoch is 116–118 of 120 in 11 of 12 runs (DeepCpG seed 17: 105); the last-10-epoch slope is still negative.
  The fixed 7,920-update budget is about 7.2% of the TCGA updates (declared deviation D4); models may be less converged than on TCGA, equally across
  arms. No extension was made.
- **Absolute MSE is not comparable with TCGA** (different cohort, tissue, variance and prior).
- **Seeds share the mask seed (17001), panels and validation order**, so they vary initialization only; per-seed CIs are not independent replicates.
- **Cohort:** whole blood (leukocyte mixture) vs pan-tumour TCGA; preprocessing differs; probe-design bias is common to all arms.
- **Fit-scope and capacity differences:** the candidate is fit on chr1–19, the functional and CpGPT stores on all loci; embedding dimensions 128/256/512
  with different adapter capacity; 846 universe loci have all-zero candidate embeddings (kept for every arm).
- **CpGPT/DeepCpG provenance:** methylation-pretrained; membership of GSE40279 in the CpGPT corpus is documented as out-of-corpus by the local provenance
  but not independently verified.
- **Experiment B (TCGA → external transfer) has not been run;** `last.pt` was not kept (deviation from protocol text §7/D6, see runner doc).
