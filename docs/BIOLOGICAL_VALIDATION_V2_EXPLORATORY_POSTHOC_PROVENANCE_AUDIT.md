## 2. Original pretraining / supervision
**CpGPT-large**
 * Pretraining config: loss weights `m_mae: 10.0, m_mae_unc: 1.0, betas_wd: 1.0, contrastive: 1.0, sample_kld: 1.0` (methylation-value reconstruction/uncertainty, Wasserstein on beta distribution, contrastive/sample losses); `binarize_input: true`, `reconstruct_mode: all`, trained on `cpgcorpus` train/val/test splits - VERIFIED-LOCAL-SIBLING (`dependencies/model/config/large.yaml`).
 * Corpus: Illumina methylation arrays only (GPL13534 450k, GPL18809 450k, GPL21145/GPL23976/GPL29753 EPIC, GPL33022 EPICv2 listed for CpGCorpus); "CpGPT was pretrained with bulk data from all of the available Illumina arrays, besides the Horvath Mammalian array" - VERIFIED-LOCAL-SIBLING (`models/CpGPT/README.md`) and VERIFIED-EXTERNAL (GitHub README, https://github.com/lcamillo/CpGPT, fetched).
 * bioRxiv preprint doi 10.1101/2024.10.24.619766 (v5) - VERIFIED-EXTERNAL (https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.10.24.619766v5.full, summarised by the fetch tool, not read in full): CpGCorpus "over 150,000 human DNA methylation samples from more than 2,000 studies" (GEO), array platforms only (27k, 450k, EPIC, EPIC+, EPICv2; ~1.17 M probes), no WGBS in the pretraining set, human bulk data across tissues/development/disease (mammalian data mostly in validation/test), 50% of CpGs masked during training, DNA flanking context (2,001 bp) embedded with Nucleotide Transformer v2 500M and projected by TRAINABLE MLPs into the CpGPT embedding space. Exact sample counts/tissue breakdown: **UNVERIFIED** (the 150k/2,000 figures are VERIFIED-EXTERNAL only through the fetch-tool summary of v5; the preprint was not read in full).
 * Consequence for the arm used here: the sequence-encoder projection (the 512-d locus vector) is a trained component of a model optimised with methylation-value losses; the frozen NTv2 input is genomic-only. Whether the locus vector itself carries methylation-derived information beyond sequence determinants is inferred from the architecture, not measured: **UNVERIFIED** as a measurement, **VERIFIED** as a training fact.

## 4. Plausible overlap with Loyfer-type targets (Loyfer 2023 atlas: WGBS, FACS-sorted primary human cell types, 39 groups, GRCh38)
 * **Direct overlap of data**: UNVERIFIED but unlikely for CpGPT (arrays vs WGBS; GEO bulk samples may include the same tissues, and some arrays of sorted blood cell types exist in GEO; whether any Loyfer/GSE186458 samples or their array-profiled counterparts are in CpGCorpus: not checked, UNVERIFIED). Not applicable for DeepCpG-DNA HCC (cancer cell line scRRBS).
 * **Direct supervision on methylation**: YES for both (training objective on methylation values/states) - VERIFIED (config / repo / external).
 * **Indirect overlap (plausible)**: CpGPT's training set is dominated by human bulk tissue arrays including blood and many solid tissues; array probes are exactly the benchmark universe (408,399 array CpGs), so the representation was optimised to reconstruct methylation at these very loci across cell/tissue types. The long-range, cell-type-specific methylation programs that Loyfer profiles measure (tissue-specific hypomethylation, cross-chromosomal co-variation) are, in principle, a learnable target of such supervision; for DeepCpG-DNA the HCC target is a single cancer-cell background (PMD-like partially methylated domain structure and CpG density determinants are shared with the primary cell types), so an indirect overlap through sequence determinants of methylation level is plausible, cell-type specificity much less so. Plausibility ranking (non-binding, UNVERIFIED as an empirical statement): CpGPT-large indirect overlap = plausible/likely; DeepCpG-DNA HCC = partial (level/CpG-density determinants only).
 * Sequence-determinism caveat: a large fraction of cross-cell-type structure in methylation profiles (CpG density, island/shore status, GC, local repeats) is sequence-derivable and can be learned without methylation labels; the arm contrast cannot attribute an advantage to supervision rather than to sequence information. The ridge targets T2/T3 of Part B separate shape from level but not supervision from sequence.
 * Documentary outcome used by the registered rule C: **PO = DIRECT_METHYLATION_SUPERVISION for both sequence arms** (fixed here; it does not depend on any result).
