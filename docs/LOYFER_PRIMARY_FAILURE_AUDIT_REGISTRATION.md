## AMENDMENT 2 (2026-10-05, after attempt 1 of step 1; nothing committed, rerun not yet started): float16 rounding-boundary check

**Observation (attempt 1, preserved read-only in `step1_target_audit_attempt1_FAIL/`, incl. `VERDICT.json` = FAIL)**: exactly one hard check failed, `group_beta_f16_rounding_mismatch_frac_ok`: 677 of 15,756,235 cells (4.2967e-05) have `round_f16(beta_f64) != frozen float16`, above the registered threshold `MAX_F16_MISMATCH_FRAC = 1e-5`. All other 25 checks passed, including `pearson_reproduces_frozen_float16_path` (max |diff| 8.9e-16 on clean pairs), `n_beyond_half_ulp = 0`, identical masks/NaN patterns/n_shared; 1,280 pairs touch a boundary cell (max |dPearson| over all pairs 1.03e-3). Unrounded-vs-frozen Pearson: max 0.00493, median 1.04e-4, Spearman 0.9999988.
**The old rule is marked `SUPERSEDED after observation`**: the a-priori 1e-5 fraction threshold was too tight (the observed 4.3e-5 exceeded it); it is NOT replaced because the data "passed", but because the fraction was never a test of the mechanism. The new rule below is justified by the mechanism diagnosed read-only on attempt 1 (`step1_diag_f16_mismatch/diagnosis_attempt1.json`), and its tolerance is derived from float32 arithmetic, not from the observed values.
**Diagnosed mechanism (read-only, from counts h5 + provenance, no heavy recomputation)**: the frozen aggregation (`loyfer_aggregate.py`) accumulates in float32 (`M/C` float32, per-donor and per-group sums float32) before the cast to float16, whereas the audit accumulates in float64. All 677 mismatch cells lie within 2.84e-04 float16 ULP (relative 1.73e-07, i.e. about 1.5 float32 unit roundoffs) of a float16 rounding midpoint; 676 distinct CpGs (max 2 cells per CpG), spread over all groups (largest: Endothel 43, Gastric-Ep 38, Head-Neck-Ep 38). float32 vs float64 betas differ by at most 2.46e-07. A diagnostic float32 re-implementation of the frozen accumulation order reproduces 15,756,227 of 15,756,235 frozen cells and 673 of the 677 mismatch cells; the 8 residual cells (also within float32 distance of a midpoint) show that the exact summation order is not fully reproduced, so **the "float32 accumulation" hypothesis is supported (not proven bit-for-bit)**; the registered rule therefore does NOT rely on exact reproduction.
**New hard rule `group_beta_f16_mismatch_within_float32_precision_of_midpoint`** (replaces the fraction check as a hard check): every cell with `round_f16(beta_f64) != frozen float16` must have beta_f64 within a RELATIVE distance **<= 64 x 2^-24 = 3.8e-06** of the nearest float16 rounding midpoint (64 = a bound on the number of float32 operations in the chain per-sample division, <=22-sample sum, donor mean, donor sum, group mean; 2^-24 = float32 unit roundoff). This is a non-circular mechanism test: a genuine aggregation error (different rule, mask, donor definition, count error) would move cells by ~1e-4..1e-3 relative, 2-3 orders above the tolerance, and is not explained (-> FAIL; tested synthetically). The observed maximum (1.73e-07) is a factor 22 below the tolerance.
All other hard checks are UNCHANGED (incl. `group_beta_within_half_ulp` with its +1e-6 slack, Pearson 1e-9 on clean pairs, masks, mapping, pairing, structural checks). The old fraction (observed vs 1e-5, `would_have_passed`) and the float32-reconstruction diagnostic stay in the results as informational fields.
**Verdict and caveat**: the verdict remains `PASS_LEVEL_A` / `FAIL`; it passes only under this amendment and remains limited as in Amendment 1 (Level B not executed: per-sample counts and `.pat` parsing not verified). The final report must state that the original 1e-5 threshold was not met (attempt 1 FAIL) and that PASS_LEVEL_A exists only thanks to Amendment 2.
**Decision tree**: BUG = step-1 verdict FAIL under the new rule. The float16 rounding-boundary case is classified **NON-BUG**; its effect on the target is bounded by the unrounded-vs-frozen comparison (max |dPearson| 0.00493, Spearman 0.9999988; rule R2(d) of section 11 still evaluates the primary on the unrounded target).
**Archiving**: attempt 1 is kept in `step1_target_audit_attempt1_FAIL/` (copy) and must be cited in the report; the rerun writes to `step1_target_audit/` (the attempt-1 original is replaced only by the rerun; the copy remains).

## 1. Conventions

## 3. Section 2: RELIABILITY / NOISE CEILING (same command, only if section 1 = PASS)

Unit of splitting = **donor** (the unit the frozen target averages over). Groups with >= 2 donors (35): donors split at random into two EQUAL halves (the odd donor dropped), `default_rng([17000, rep])`, rep = 0..19 (**20 repetitions**).
Each half gets a group-beta profile per CpG with the frozen rule (valid sample cov >= 10; donor mean; unweighted mean over donors with a valid beta; a half-group is observed iff >= 1 valid donor). Pair similarity per half = Pearson over pairwise-complete groups, >= ceil(G_used*20/39) shared (18 for 35 groups), non-constant.
Pair set = frozen pairs minus D2, defined in both halves. Reliability r = Spearman(T_halfA, T_halfB) (Pearson also stored) for: all, six distance strata, intra, inter, V_Q1..V_Q4 and V_top10pct (edges reused from the follow-up B1: 0.000369276 | 0.00133439 | 0.00841199 | 0.0266948), and the primary-style pooled statistic (weighted Spearman of A vs B).
**Spearman-Brown** (labelled, k = 2): rho = 2r/(1+r). **Empirical ceiling for the primary** = sqrt(max(rho_pooled, 0)) (classical attenuation bound on the correlation of any predictor of the true similarity with the observed target; descriptive). Median and 2.5/97.5 percentiles over the 20 repetitions are reported.
Single-sample (single-donor) groups cannot be split: excluded from the split-half and listed separately; sensitivity variant "groups with >= 4 donors" (21 groups, min_shared scaled the same way).

## 4. Sections 3-4: TARGET SEMANTICS and PAIR-LIST AUDIT (step `semantics-pairs`)

CpG profile = frozen primary-mask betas. Per CpG: mean M, variance V (ddof 0), range R = max - min, n groups. Pair: V = min(V_i,V_j), R = min(R_i,R_j), mean = (M_i+M_j)/2, n informative groups = `n_shared_groups`. All on D2-kept pairs.
Bands of the target Pearson: **top 1%** (>= q99), **bottom 1%** (<= q01), **median** (q49.5..q50.5), plus all. Examples: 6 per band, the first 6 of a seeded permutation (`default_rng([17, band])`) of the band members; profiles tabulated in CSV (no manual selection; plots made in phase 2 from the table).
Indicators (quantile levels fixed now, values recorded at run time): near-constant: pair V < 0.000369276 (the B1 V_Q1 edge); small-amplitude: pair R < R_small = **25th percentile of R over the D2-kept pairs**; global-level: both CpGs have R2_L >= G_hi = **75th percentile** of the per-CpG squared Pearson R2_L between the CpG profile and the global group-level vector L (L_g = mean beta of group g over all CpGs observed in g; CpGs with >= 20 groups).
Hierarchical, mutually exclusive classes per pair (first match): NEAR_CONSTANT, SMALL_AMPLITUDE, GLOBAL_LEVEL, SPECIFIC_PROGRAM. Also: Pearson after regressing both profiles on L over the shared groups ("persists" if same sign and >= 0.5 x original).
Class shares per band and per stratum, medians/IQR of the indicators per band. **Dominance rule R2(c)**: top-1% band is dominated by uninformative pairs iff share(NEAR_CONSTANT u SMALL_AMPLITUDE) >= 0.50 AND >= 1.5 x its share among all pairs.
Pair-list audit per stratum (six strata) of the same pairs: pair-mean and |difference| of M, V, R, GC, CpG density (+hg38), TSS distance, peak counts (total / histone / DNase = number of selected tracks overlapped, CSR only), context (same-context share, context shares), chromosome composition (counts, Jensen-Shannon distance vs reference), zero-row exclusion per stratum, target distribution;
effect sizes: standardised mean difference (Cohen d, pooled sd) and two-sample KS of every stratum **vs the reference set = pooled >1Mb + interchromosomal**, plus the full 6x6 SMD/KS matrices. "Breadth" is not available separately (the dense block is never read): the number of overlapped tracks IS the breadth proxy.
Why the arm ranking differs between short and long range (step `baselines`, mediation): per arm and stratum Spearman(target, cosine) and the partial Spearman controlling for [-|dM|, -|dGC|, -|dpeak_total|] (and -log10 distance for intra strata), plus Spearman of target/cosine with -|dGC| and -|dpeak|.

## 7. Section 7: SVD GEOMETRY (step `svd-geometry`)

Embedding-space PCA (not the compressor's component directions) over the 407,550 D2 universe loci (as B2): mu, exact `eigh` covariance, axes sorted by eigenvalue (largest |component| positive). First 20 PCs: **Spearman** (across the 407,550 loci) of the PC score with total / histone / DNase peak count, GC, CpG density (both), TSS distance, Loyfer mean, variance and range; eta^2 with context; the same for the first 20 raw embedding coordinates (the SVD component axes).
Diagnostic curve of the primary-style statistic (+ per stratum, V_top10pct): native cosine; centred cosine (k = 0); cosine after removing the top k PCs (z = e - mu, project out V[:, :k]; k = 1, 2, 5, 10); per-coordinate standardised cosine; whitened cosine (PCA whitening, (lambda + 1e-6 lambda_max)^-1/2, label-free); negative Euclidean; plus the Spearman of the target with each PC's centred dot-product contribution s_ik s_jk (k = 1..20). Zero-norm residuals raise. Consistency with B2 (centred, PC1-removed) recorded.
Purely diagnostic: no number of PCs is chosen as a representation.

## 11. Section 11: DECISION TREE (operational rules; thresholds fixed now; implemented in `decision.py`, evaluated by `report-inputs`)

Constants: M_REC = 0.03 (Spearman units); NULL_HI as in section 5; "X recovers the target" iff X >= NULL_HI(arm) + M_REC.
* **BUG**: step-1 verdict FAIL (any hard Level-A check above, incl. pairing/coordinate errors). A non-FAIL verdict (`PASS_LEVEL_A`) means "no bug downstream of the per-sample counts"; it does NOT exclude errors in the counts or in the `.pat` parsing (Level B not executed, Amendment 1).
* **TARGET_LIMITATION** if ANY of: (a) empirical ceiling of the primary (median over repetitions, donors >= 2 variant) < **0.30**; (b) Spearman-Brown reliability in `>1Mb` OR `interchromosomal` < **0.50**; (c) the dominance rule R2(c) of section 4; (d) the primary statistic recomputed on the unrounded target differs by > **0.02** from the frozen value for any arm, or the arm ordering changes. Sub-rules are reported separately.
* **COMPRESSION_GEOMETRY**: the candidate's native 256-d cosine does NOT recover (native < NULL_HI + 0.03) AND at least one of {raw binary cosine, raw Jaccard (section 6), OOF-T1/T2 pair similarity under the original or the extended grid (sections 8-9)} recovers AND its paired block-bootstrap delta vs the native cosine on the same pair set has CI lower bound > 0. Supporting flag (not required): a section-7 curve entry with delta vs native >= 0.03 and CI > 0.
* **INFORMATION_ABSENCE**: none of {raw binary cosine, raw Jaccard, OOF-T1/T2 original, OOF-T1/T2 extended} recovers. Mutually exclusive with COMPRESSION_GEOMETRY (asserted).
* **COMPARATOR_ADVANTAGE**: for a comparator in {CpGPT-large, DeepCpG-DNA}: in EVERY fair evaluation F = {native cosine, centred cosine, PC1-removed, PC1-10-removed, whitened cosine, negative Euclidean (section 7), OOF-T1 original, OOF-T1 extended} the paired block-bootstrap CI of (comparator - candidate) lies above 0 AND the comparator recovers (>= its NULL_HI + 0.03) = STRONG; native plus >= 1 but not all = PARTIAL; else NONE. The class holds iff some comparator is STRONG.
Several classes can coexist; `report-inputs` writes the flags with all evidence; no free-text interpretation. Thresholds cannot be tuned after seeing results.
