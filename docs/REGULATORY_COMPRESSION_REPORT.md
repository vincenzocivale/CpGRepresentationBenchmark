# Regulatory compression report (Step 5)

Representation properties only. No methylation data and no benchmark performance were used or are reported here.
Generated by `scripts/build_regulatory_embeddings.py` (config `configs/regulatory_embeddings.yaml`) and
`scripts/report_regulatory_embeddings.py` (JSON, `spectra.png`, `block_contribution.png`, `report.md` in
`outputs/regulatory_compression_reports/`). Code: `src/cpg_repr_benchmark/encode_atlas/compression.py`.

## Protocol
- Input: binary peak-overlap CSR (`track_indptr`/`track_indices`) of the TCGA-array feature store, 408,399 loci. Only `cpg_idx`,
  `track_indptr`, `track_indices` are read (`/dense` is refused by `TrackStore._read`; verified in every manifest).
- Fit protocol `discovery_chr1_19`: fit on chr1-19 (388,599 loci, sha256 `995edc58...ef120bc`); chr20-22 (19,800 loci,
  sha256 `9345a51b...dfdd2`) are transformed only. Seed 17, randomized TruncatedSVD (n_iter 7, oversamples 20), float32.
- Uncentered SVD of binary tracks, no column scaling. Sign convention: largest-|loading| entry of each component positive.
- block_svd: per-block SVD (histone / tf / accessibility, up to 256 comps), each block rescaled so its mean squared row norm over the
  fit loci is 1 (post Frobenius^2 per locus = 1.0 for all blocks in all stores), concatenated, then a label-free 256-d SVD.
- `_egm` = replicate_weighting `equal_group_mass` (column weight 1/sqrt(n) per (assay, biosample, target) group; no track dropped).
  regulatory_clean: 4151 tracks in 2797 groups.
- Stored embeddings are float32, shape (408,399, 256), 421 MB each; compressor npz 4-9 MB. Not registered as benchmark arms.
- Fit time (wall, shared loaded machine): global 680-1085 s, block 4137-4560 s.

## Properties (fit loci chr1-19)
E = explained energy (uncentered; block_svd: share of the energy of the block-normalized concatenation). V = centered variance share
(global only). PR / expH = participation-ratio / exp-entropy effective rank of the centered covariance of the 256-d embedding.
Block-only = share of embedding energy from each block's tracks alone (cross terms excluded, so does not sum to 1).

Notes
- All spectra are dominated by a few components (the first component is overall peak density, since SVD is uncentered). Effective
  rank is low (PR 3-6, expH 15-26 of 256 dims), so most of the 256 dimensions carry little energy; this is a property of the
  representation, not an error.
- Global SVD is histone dominated (72% block-only energy for clean); block_svd equalizes the three blocks by construction (~1/3 each).
- Row norms: global median 13.2 (fit) vs 15.3 (chr20-22); block 0.94 vs 1.11; i.e. chr20-22 rows are ~15-20% larger in norm
  (denser annotation), a distribution shift to keep in mind but no collapse. Zero rows: 0.18% (fit), 0.15% (chr20-22) of loci
  have no track overlap at all (embedding exactly 0) for the clean sets (0.29%/0.24% for histone).
- clean vs all_experimental are essentially identical (14 extra tracks; all properties match to 3 decimals).
- none vs equal_group_mass (regulatory_clean): global principal-angle cosines mean 0.83 (166/256 > 0.9; leading-32 subspaces 0.96);
  block mean 0.77 (152/256 > 0.9; leading-32 0.94). Weighting shifts energy from accessibility to histone/tf in the global SVD
  (dnase block-only 0.14 -> 0.09), raises effective rank (global expH 18.4 -> 20.0, block expH 21.7 -> 26.4, PR 5.1 -> 5.7) and
  lowers row norm (13.2 -> 10.6). Subspaces are largely shared at the top, and diverge in the tail.
