# Figure 3. Predicting EWAS Catalog-associated CpGs

Absolute pooled out-of-fold AUROC for the prospectively frozen 13-task EWAS-v3 panel: (A) Cancer, five tasks; (B) Immune/inflammatory, four tasks; (C) Cardiometabolic, four tasks. Histone+DNase is the frozen regulatory_histone_dnase_v1 representation (256 dimensions), CpGPT-large uses native 512 dimensions, and DeepCpG-DNA uses native 128 dimensions. Primary positive sets are EWAS Catalog-associated CpGs at p < 1e-4, intersected with the fixed 408,399-CpG universe. The endpoint labels explicitly distinguish incident and prevalent analyses; Crohn's disease is the single frozen IBD-family representative.

Bars show absolute AUROC and begin at 0.5, the dashed chance reference. Black error bars are 95% percentile intervals from 1,000 paired chromosome-block bootstrap draws. All arms use identical frozen positive/background pairs, chromosome folds and bootstrap draws. Evaluation uses a nested L2 logistic probe with StandardScaler fitted within each inner/outer training fold. Matching is 1:1 without replacement, exact chromosome × Infinium probe type, seed 17; the registered EWAS-v2 design has no pair cap. AUPRC and strict matched-pair concordance are reported separately.

† denotes a positive set sharing >50% of the smaller set with another selected task. Five cardiometabolic pairs meet this criterion; the four marked tasks are retained and cannot be interpreted as independent replications. Selected endpoints can also share an underlying study/cohort; CpG overlap alone does not establish participant independence. There is no category-averaged AUROC or category win count.

The source is the official EWAS Catalog Zenodo fallback, with incomplete coverage of the complete current Catalog. Catalog StudyIDs are unavailable; source_analysis_id, Zenodo record, original filename and PMID are retained in the frozen manifest. The p < 1e-7 high-confidence EWAS-associated sets and genomic-context matching analyses are sensitivities and are not shown here.

Source data: `data/registry/ewas_v3_results/main_results.csv` (39 rows). Paired contrasts: `main_paired_contrasts.csv` in the same directory. Panel: `configs/ewas_v3/main_trait_panel.yaml`, freeze tag `ewas-v3-main-panel-freeze-v1`. Redundancy: `data/registry/ewas_v3/primary_overlap_flags.csv`.
